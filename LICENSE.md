# Why This License

- **Credibility.** We replaced the previous hand-written "Hermes Non-Commercial License"
  with ELv2, a license drafted by lawyers and already used by established infrastructure
  projects (Elastic, Couchbase, and others). What we permit and what we don't is now
  precise and defensible.
- **Honesty.** Hermes is not described as "open source" anywhere in the repository or docs.
  It is **source-available**: you can read it, fork it, modify it, and run it, under ELv2.
- **Protecting the cloud business.** The ELv2 hosted/managed-service restriction is the
  core protection: no third party can stand up a "cloud Hermes" and resell it. Hosting
  Hermes as a service is a Hermes Enterprise privilege.
- **Adoption-friendly.** Individuals, researchers, startups, and enterprises can download,
  use, modify, and redistribute the software freely. The license does not count employees
  or revenue; the ELv2 use rights are unconditional unless you host it as a service or
  strip the notices.
